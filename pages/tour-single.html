Tour Overview

Experience Hawaii's most spectacular underwater world on this unforgettable 4-hour Ko Olina adventure. Departing from one of Oahu's most picturesque marinas, you'll cruise along the island's stunning west coast in search of wild spinner dolphins before dropping anchor in a sheltered snorkeling cove teeming with marine life.

Our expert naturalist guides will lead you through vibrant coral reefs where Hawaiian green sea turtles, colorful tropical fish, and the occasional octopus call home. Whether you're a first-time snorkeler or a seasoned diver, our small-group format ensures a personal, unhurried experience at every stop.

All snorkeling equipment is provided and thoroughly cleaned between trips. A pre-water safety briefing gets everyone comfortable before you slip below the surface into Ko Olina's world-class visibility — often exceeding 60 feet on clear days.

Important Information

What to Bring

Reef-safe sunscreen, swimwear, towel, change of clothes, motion sickness medication if needed, and a camera or waterproof case.

Age & Fitness

Open to guests 5 years and older. No swimming experience required — flotation devices and guides are always available. Pregnant guests advised to consult their physician.

Cancellation Policy

Full refund for cancellations made 48 hours or more before departure. No refund within 48 hours, however we are happy to reschedule your booking once.

Weather Policy

Hawaii Nautical reserves the right to cancel trips due to unsafe conditions. All guests receive a full refund or complimentary reschedule in the event of a weather cancellation.
